About this edition

A complete digital dictionary

This site reproduces the entries of the Dictionary of Gnosis & Western Esotericism, edited by Wouter J. Hanegraaff in collaboration with Antoine Faivre, Roelof van den Broek and Jean-Pierre Brach (Brill, Leiden & Boston, 2006).

How the text was made

Every entry was extracted from the 1,186 pages of the dictionary's text layer, rebuilt from the two-column page layout, and checked against the printed List of Entries. Running heads and page numbers are omitted; the wording, bibliographies and author credits are kept in full.

The source PDF uses legacy fonts for some Arabic, Greek and Hebrew transliterations. A few glyphs in the extracted text may therefore appear as unusual characters (for example ≠ or ™). They are preserved rather than silently guessed at.
